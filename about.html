Hi!

I’m Katherine, a current sophomore studying Statistics & Data Science at UCLA!

My passion for design comes from my love for all types of art---I’ve taken art classes since the age of 7, picked up embroidery, crochet, sewing, and clay work as hobbies, and even ran a jewelry business in high school. I believe something handmade and designed with care will always have more meaning than something mass-produced that can be bought in a store. This DIY attitude is the reason every person in my life receives a hand-painted card for every birthday, and also motivates me to design with empathy and intentionality.

Outside of design, I’m also passionate about data science. Similarly to design, I’m interested by how it bridges the intersection of tech and human behavior by ultimately being rooted in discovering insights in any field.

When I’m not designing, you can find me eating at restaurants to rank on Beli, smiling with all 24 of my teeth in photobooth strips, fighting to snag concert tickets, or taking photos of my friends on my most prized possession (a hand-me-down Sony RX100 II from my mom). Thanks for stopping by!
